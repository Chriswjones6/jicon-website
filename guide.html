The Renovation
Planning Guide

Everything you should think through before you start a remodel — from budgeting to hiring — from the team at JICO Construction.

1 Get clear on your goals

Before you talk pricing, get specific about what "done" looks like. The clearer your vision, the more accurate every estimate will be.

  • What problem are you solving — space, function, looks, resale, or all of it?
  • Which rooms or areas are in scope (and which are off-limits)?
  • Must-haves vs. nice-to-haves — be honest about the difference
  • Do you plan to stay long-term, or is this to sell? It changes the math.

2 Set a realistic budget

3 Your pre-project checklist

  • Collect inspiration photos (Instagram, Pinterest, magazines)
  • Measure the space and note what you want to keep vs. change
  • Decide your firm budget and your stretch budget
  • List your must-have finishes and fixtures
  • Note your ideal start date and any hard deadlines
  • Plan for living around the work (or staying elsewhere)

4 10 questions to ask any contractor

  • Are you licensed and insured? (Ask to see proof.)
  • Is the work covered by a written contract and warranty?
  • Who is my day-to-day point of contact?
  • Will you handle permits and inspections?
  • What's the payment schedule — and is there an upfront deposit?
  • Do you use your own crew or subcontractors?
  • What's a realistic timeline, start to finish?
  • How do you handle changes and unexpected issues?
  • Can I see recent local projects or references?
  • How do you keep the site clean and safe?
Red flags: no written contract, cash-only, huge upfront deposits, no license/insurance, or pressure to "decide today." A trustworthy contractor welcomes these questions.
