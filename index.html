Who we are

Quality meets trust.

JICON Construction was founded in 2021 on a simple idea: renovations should be handled with the same care we'd give our own homes. Our roots run back more than a decade in property acquisition and renovation — so we understand a project from both sides: the craftsmanship and the investment.

We're a licensed and insured general contractor specializing in residential and commercial renovations throughout the Grand Strand. From the first walkthrough to the final cleanup, we lead with three things:

  • IntegrityWe do what we say — even when it's the harder, more expensive right thing.
  • HonestyStraight answers on scope, timeline and cost. No moving targets.
  • TransparencyYou see the plan, the pricing and the progress at every step.
